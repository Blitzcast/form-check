Runs in your browser on a laptop or phone. Your video never leaves your device.

How it works

  1. Set up side-on

    Prop your phone or laptop 2 to 3 metres away so it sees your whole body from the side.

  2. It finds your joints

    A pose model running in your browser marks your shoulder, hip, knee, ankle and heel, 30 times a second.

  3. It measures each rep

    A rep counts when your knee bends past 110° and you stand back up. Angles are measured through the whole rep.

  4. You get feedback per rep

    Each rep gets three checks, so you can see which one to work on next set.

What it checks

Depth
Your hip drops to knee height or lower at the bottom of the rep.
Chest up
Your torso stays within 45° of upright.
Heels down
Your heels stay on the floor instead of rising onto your toes.

This is an early version

The rules above are first guesses. They haven't been checked against a coach's judgement yet, so it will sometimes flag a good rep or miss a bad one. Use it as practice feedback, not medical or injury advice.

What it doesn't do yet

  • Exercises other than the bodyweight squat.
  • Front-view checks, like knees caving in.
  • More than one person in the frame.
  • Save or upload your video. It only ever sees the live picture.
